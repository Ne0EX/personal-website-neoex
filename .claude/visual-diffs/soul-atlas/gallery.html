SBA-1 §2.4 / spec §5.1 (enriched 2026-05-29) — α is not a static "Bangkok node." When Peat is in Japan, α relocates to Tokyo or Kyoto. The six archive nodes (003 Kyoto · 002 Chiang Mai · 001 Yirgacheffe · 000 SF · 012 Tokyo · 047 Point Nemo) are the opposite: fixed GPS coordinates of past record-making — "where it was surveyed." α = "here, now."
