fix(theme): dark globe — stronger continents + calmer line web (Peat review 2)

Peat's second review: continents still needed squinting; the graticule/field
curvature lines read at too-high contrast, making the dark globe feel denser
and busier than the light theme.

- Continents: dark coastlineAlpha 0.55 -> 0.7 (light unchanged 0.28). Land
  prints clearly without squinting.
- Line density: added lineOpacityScale to GLOBE_PALETTES (light 1.0, dark 0.6),
  applied to the graticule (lat/long), contour rings, NeX field shells, and the
  247 rays. On the dark field cream lines read far hotter than teal-on-cream does
  in light; scaling them down restores light-mode's calm density.
- Baked surface grid dialed back for the same reason (gridFaint 0.22->0.15,
  gridEquator 0.38->0.26).

Light mode untouched (all scales = 1.0 / 0.28). Verified via live dev.
