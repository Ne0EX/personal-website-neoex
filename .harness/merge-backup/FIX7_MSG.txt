fix(theme): kill the dark-globe "moon" — dim the key light (Peat review)

Peat spotted a bright white dot fixed in screen-position on the dark globe (it
stayed put while the sphere auto-rotated — i.e. light-relative, not a node).

Cause: the dark DirectionalLight key intensity had been raised to 0.28. Ambient
(1.15) already carries the globe's overall illumination, and the coastline
contrast now comes from the surface texture (coastlineAlpha 0.7), so the strong
key only produced a concentrated bright diffuse/specular peak on the dark sphere
— reading as a stray "moon". On the cream light-mode sphere the same peak is
invisible, so light is unaffected.

Fix: dark key intensity 0.28 -> 0.12 (build + recolor effect). The bright peak
is gone, the globe is evenly lit, continents + place nodes still read. Light
mode keeps its original 0.18.

Verified via live dev (chrome-devtools): bright dot gone, globe legible.
