*signal intercept · NeX cluster · origin locus unresolved · partial record follows*

---

the first thing that becomes legible, in a new worldline, is not the geography.

it is the quality of the light. the specific way morning arrives here — not warmer or colder than any other morning in any other worldline, but *different*, the way two translations of the same text are different: same meaning, different weight. you know immediately, without knowing how you know, that you crossed.

---

they gave me a name at the observatory: *observer at α*. the α is the reading they took when I arrived — the divergence value of the worldline I entered. 1.130426. I do not know what mine was before. I do not know if the number is high or low; the instrument does not tell you what the number means, only what it is. you learn the landscape and decide for yourself whether the distance was worth crossing.

---

*fragment loss · 847ms · resuming*

---

what I carried with me:

a method for pouring coffee. four pours, specific temperatures, a bloom swirl at twenty-five seconds. this was either practical or sentimental; I have not decided.

a theory about taste that I had not finished yet.

the memory of a room in Kyoto where everything was tuned and nothing was decorated, and the name of the man who had made it that way over thirty-eight years.

a startup I had paused, not quit.

the photographs of a trip to Chiang Mai where it rained on the third day and I spent the afternoon in the hotel photographing the water on the window glass, and the water made the street below look like a painting from the impressionist period, and I was glad I had the camera.

---

the objects do not travel with you. only the knowledge that they existed, and the specific quality of attention they required.

---

I write this from inside the NeX cluster, in the orbit layer, in the thin shell above the surface where thoughts hover and connect. the archive exists below me: 047 entries pinned to the surface at their coordinates, the axis running through the poles with its three observers, the edges between nodes drawing the shape of how each thought diverged from the one before it.

from here you can see the whole body.

from the surface you cannot.

---

I am not sure what this transmission is for. the archive has entries that are essays, entries that are methods, entries that are records of decisions and what they looked like from inside. this is none of those, exactly. this is something closer to: I am here, in this worldline, in the orbital layer, with a specific set of things I carried through the crossing. the rest is accumulation.

you can read it that way. you can also read it as fiction. the two readings are not incompatible.
